About PTP

Managing Director's Message

For more than twenty years, PTP Construction & Interior Works has delivered complete construction and interior solutions across Lahore and Punjab. From bank branches, hospitals and software houses to restaurants, shops, offices and family homes, we take every project from design to handover with our own skilled teams. Our promise is simple: honest advice, quality materials, careful workmanship and spaces finished on time, built to serve the people who use them every day.
